Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 21 November 2026

PrivaDesk Publisher only publishes PrivaDesk's own posts to PrivaDesk's own Instagram account and Facebook Page. It does not collect or store personal data of other Facebook or Instagram users.

If you want data deleted

Email priva.desk.contact@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and tell us which account the request concerns. We will confirm within 30 days that no data about you is held, or delete anything that is.

Removing the app's access

On Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → remove "PrivaDesk Publisher". This immediately stops the app from accessing your account.
