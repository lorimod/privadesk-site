Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers PrivaDesk Publisher, the internal tool PrivaDesk uses to publish its own posts to its own Instagram account (@priva.desk) and its own Facebook Page. It does not cover the PrivaDesk hardware products, which are in development.

What the tool does

The tool uploads images and videos that PrivaDesk created, with their captions, to PrivaDesk's own Instagram account and Facebook Page through Meta's official APIs, at scheduled times. It reads basic statistics (such as reach and likes) of PrivaDesk's own posts.

Data we collect about you

None. The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any Instagram or Facebook user. It only acts on accounts owned by PrivaDesk.

Data the tool handles

  • PrivaDesk's own access token for its own accounts, stored privately and never shared.
  • PrivaDesk's own media files, kept in a private storage bucket only until they are published, then deleted.
  • Public identifiers of PrivaDesk's own published posts (post IDs and links), kept to avoid posting twice.

Sharing

We do not share data with third parties. Media is sent only to Meta, as required to publish it.
